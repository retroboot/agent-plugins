# ADR-005: Research runs in every branch, the Facilitator starts it again, one summary line shows

**Status:** accepted (developer, 2026-11-24)

**Proposed by:** developer

## Context

`brainstorm` Phase 2 is silent today (`skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md:76`); every other dispatch prints a
line per role. The pack left open what the developer sees, who starts research in Phase 4, and
whether a read-only resume researches (prd **Checkpoint answers**, A10, A11 and A12).

## Options

1. **Keep Phase 2 silent.** Rejected: the developer could not tell research ran. **A line per role.**
   Rejected: three lines before the opening crowd it. **One summary line.** Chosen.
2. **Research in Phase 4 only when the developer asks, or with a cap.** Rejected: the developer
   should not have to notice new ground first, and nothing fixes a cap. **The Facilitator decides,
   no cap, and says when it starts.** Chosen.
3. **Skip research on a read-only resume.** Rejected: the Brief names no exception and the
   conversation still runs. **Research runs there too.** Chosen.

## Decision

Phase 2 shows one summary line for the research step. In Phase 4 the Facilitator starts research
when the conversation reaches new ground, with no cap, and says so. Research runs in every Phase 1
branch that reaches the conversation, read-only resumes included.

## Consequences

The opening stays uncluttered and research stays visible. Research cost grows with the
conversation, and a read-only resume pays for research it cannot save.
